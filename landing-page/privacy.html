Privacy Policy

Ekadashi is a simple reminder app for Ekadashi dates. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Notifications

The app asks for notification permission only so it can remind you before each Ekadashi. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Data

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The app only downloads the public Ekadashi dates list; no personal information is sent.

Contact

Questions? Reach us via the app's Google Play page.
